## Selected Projects

### [Multilingual RAG for Korean University Administrative Documents](https://github.com/showlaoyuan/multilingual-korean-university-rag)

A research project that makes Korean university administrative documents easier to search with Chinese and Korean questions. The current pipeline combines multilingual embeddings, school-filtered Top-5 retrieval, LLM-based Chinese answer generation, and source, page, and chunk references. A frozen 30-question bilingual baseline (13 Correct, 7 Partial, 10 Wrong), evidence-level error analysis, and a documented negative retrieval experiment support the ongoing graduation thesis work.

### [Financial NLP Data and Evaluation](https://github.com/showlaoyuan/capstone_financial_nlp)

Started as a team capstone focused on financial NLP data preparation and evaluation. The repository documents the data and evaluation work I handled in that project, followed by an independent reorganization and extension after the capstone. The completed workflow covers four datasets, prompt A/B tests, two API model-and-prompt configurations, result records, and reproducibility documentation. A total of 14,755 records were processed and validated; the model comparison used a fixed 800-record pilot set and reports accuracy, format compliance, latency, and estimated cost. Detailed contribution boundaries and limitations remain in the project documentation.

### [Hansorri — Co-founder, Product & Chinese Localization](https://hansorri.com/)

A live Korean-to-Chinese classroom translation product currently used by customers in Korea. Hansorri supports real-time lecture translation, online-class and audio-file translation, searchable course archives, exportable bilingual notes, and picture-in-picture captions. I work on product planning, Chinese localization, UI/UX feedback, testing, and iteration alongside the team developing the translation engine and application.

[Official website](https://hansorri.com/) · [Available on the Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/id6764813419)
